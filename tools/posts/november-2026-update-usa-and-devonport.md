Hi everyone, Nick here.

We're almost halfway through November, so it felt like the right time to sit down and share what's been happening at Nexus. It's been a busy few weeks, and a really good one.

## We're now supplying skilled trades to the USA

At the start of November we began supplying people to projects in the United States. For a business built in Plymouth, that's a big step, and I'll be honest, I expected the first month to be about finding our feet.

It hasn't worked out that way. Two weeks in, we've already got people on site into double figures across three states: **Mississippi, Louisiana and Florida**.

Every one of them was vetted and ticketed before they travelled, and every one knew exactly what the job, the hours and the pay looked like before they said yes. That's the part that matters to me. Getting people out there is one thing. Getting the right people there, ready to work from day one, is what our clients rely on us for.

## More US roles to fill

The demand isn't slowing down. We're recruiting for more than 60 welders and fitters across the three states right now:

- **Mississippi:** pipe fitters, a pipe supervisor, structural plate fitters and structural welders
- **Louisiana:** structural welders, aluminium welders, a pipe fitter and a pipe welder
- **Florida:** pipe fitters, pipe welders, plate and structural fitters and structural welders

![A welder at work in a Gulf Coast shipyard](/assets/img/blog-us-welding.webp "Welding on the Gulf Coast. U.S. Navy photo by Austin Rooney.")

The terms are $19 an hour on 70-hour weeks, with a $20 daily per diem, paid every two weeks, on 3 to 5 month contracts.

If you're a welder or fitter and a few months' work in the States appeals, [take a look at the live roles](/candidates/#vacancies) or send me a message on WhatsApp.

## Closer to home: more labour into Devonport

Devonport is where I started out as an apprentice back in 2004, so this one means a lot to me. We've increased the number of people we supply into Devonport for Denholm Industrial Services, and they've asked us for more.

In this industry, a repeat order is the best feedback you can get. It means the people we sent turned up, did the job properly and made life easier for the team around them. That's down to them, and I'm proud of every one of them.

## What I'm looking forward to

Over the next few weeks I'm looking forward to filling the US roles, building out the team at Devonport and welcoming a lot of new faces to Nexus. There are also one or two things in the pipeline that I can't talk about just yet. Watch this space.
