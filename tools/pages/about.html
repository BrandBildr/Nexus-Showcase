About Nexus

There when your project needs us most.

Nexus Resourcing Group puts skilled, vetted people onto commercial, domestic, marine and defence projects across the UK and worldwide. It's built on more than twenty years of resourcing experience, and on one idea: the best time to solve a labour gap is before it opens.

Why Nexus exists

Most projects don't fail for lack of a plan. They slip because the right people aren't there when the work is ready for them.

A fitter who doesn't turn up. A welder whose tickets have lapsed. A crew called in the night before and inducted on the morning they start. Anyone who has run a yard, a site or a programme knows the cost of those gaps, and how often resourcing is treated as a last-minute phone call.

Nexus was set up to do it differently. We get involved early, learn the job and the site, and build a bench of proven people around your programme. So when the work steps up, or something changes at short notice, the right people are ready to go.

A fresh take on resourcing

The usual way waits for a problem. The Nexus way plans for the work.

The usual way

  • You call when the gap appears
  • CVs sent in bulk, vetting left to you
  • A different account manager every time
  • Contact stops once the invoice is raised

The Nexus way

  • We plan people around your programme from day one
  • Tickets, references and right to work checked before anyone is put forward
  • One direct line to the person finding your people
  • We stay in touch for the life of the project

Meet Nick

20+ years resourcing the marine industry

Nick has spent more than two decades putting skilled people onto marine projects. He has seen resourcing from every side: the yard waiting on a crew, the tradesperson waiting on a start date, and the programme manager trying to hold the two together.

He knows the pressure points first hand. Late call-ups. Unvetted labour. Programmes slipping while roles sit empty. Nexus is his answer to them: a business that works ahead of your programme, knows its people personally, and stays close once they're on site.

When you call Nexus, you talk to Nick. No call centre and no hand-offs. The person you speak to is the person who finds your people.

Good resourcing shouldn't start when something goes wrong. It should start when the job is planned.

Safety and compliance, done properly

Every person we put forward has been checked before they reach your site. You get the paperwork with them, not after them.

  • Right to work and ID

    Identity and right to work in the UK verified for every placement.

  • Tickets and qualifications

    Trade cards and certifications checked as current, such as CSCS, ECS and CCNSG, plus coded welding and specialist tickets.

  • References and track record

    Previous work and references confirmed, so you know who is arriving.

  • Site readiness

    PPE, inductions and site rules briefed before day one, not on it.

  • Security vetting support

    For defence work, we support candidates through BPSS and SC clearance with your security team.

  • Ongoing contact

    Regular check-ins with you and your people, so issues are dealt with early.
