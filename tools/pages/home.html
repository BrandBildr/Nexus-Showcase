Meet Nick

22 years in marine and defence engineering

Nick started as an apprentice at Devonport Royal Dockyard in 2004 and spent 22 years at Babcock, rising to Project Manager and Head of Department. He knows the pressure points first hand: late call-ups, unvetted labour, and programmes slipping while roles sit empty.

Nexus is his different take on resourcing. Rather than waiting for a vacancy to become a problem, Nick works ahead of your programme, building the right people around the project before the gap appears.

Good resourcing shouldn't start when something goes wrong. It should start when the job is planned.
