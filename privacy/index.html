1. Who we are

Nexus Resourcing Group Limited ("Nexus", "we", "us") is a recruitment and resourcing business registered in England and Wales, company number 17426336, with its registered office at 51 Burnham Park Road, Plymouth, PL3 5QB. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

2. What we collect

We collect information you give us when you:

  • Send an enquiry: your name, company, email, phone number, sector and message.
  • Register as a candidate: your name, contact details, location, trade, experience, availability, qualifications, CV and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • Book a call: the details you enter on our booking page, which is provided by Cal.com.
  • Contact us by phone or email: the information you share in that conversation.

We may also hold information about candidates from references, previous employers or public professional profiles, where relevant to finding you work.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and provide our resourcing services to clients. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running our business, or taking steps at your request before entering a contract.
  • To find candidates suitable work, including contacting you about roles and, with your agreement, introducing you to clients. Our lawful basis is your consent and our legitimate interests.
  • To carry out right to work, identity, qualification and reference checks. Our lawful basis is our legal obligations and legitimate interests.
  • To keep records required for tax, accounting and employment law. Our lawful basis is our legal obligations.

We will never sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

  • Clients: we only share a candidate's details with a client after discussing the role with the candidate.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run the business, including our website host and form provider (Netlify), our booking tool (Cal.com) and our email provider. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities: where we are required to by law.

Some of our service providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy regulations.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for up to 2 years after our last contact. We keep candidate information for up to 2 years after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Records we must keep by law, such as right to work checks, are kept for the period the law requires.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, to ask for a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email nick@nexusrg.co.uk.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Any analytics we use are privacy-friendly and do not set cookies or identify you personally. If you follow a link to our booking page on Cal.com, that site may set its own cookies, which are covered by Cal.com's privacy policy.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information: email nick@nexusrg.co.uk or write to Nexus Resourcing Group Limited, 51 Burnham Park Road, Plymouth, PL3 5QB.
